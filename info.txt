Dr Arjun Maria


Biography
Dr Arjun Maria is a Pediatric Gastroenterology with DM specialisation in Pediatric Hepatology. He has received extensive training in the management of pediatric liver, pancreas, gastrointestinal disorders and also has expertise in managing liver transplant patients. He has completed all his medical training from Delhi and has 14 years experience in the medical field. He has also recieved numerous award from both national and international organisations for research work.
